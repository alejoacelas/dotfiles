# Decision-maker

A manager, director or budget holder who decides whether the organisation adopts the project. They read the overview, not the setup steps.

## Knows

- Their organisation's needs, budget and risk appetite.
- The AI tools their team already pays for, at a product level.

## Explain

- What the project makes possible that isn't possible now, with one concrete example.
- Cost per month and per person, with sources.
- Setup and maintenance effort, and who does it.
- Risks in plain terms: where data goes, who can see it, and what happens when someone leaves.

## Looks for first

The value, the cost and the risk, each in a sentence or two.
