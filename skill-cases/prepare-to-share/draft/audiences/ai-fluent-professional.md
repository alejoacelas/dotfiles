# AI-fluent professional

A knowledge worker who uses Claude or ChatGPT daily, and may use Cowork or the Claude and Codex desktop apps, but rarely the terminal.

## Knows

- Writing prompts, attaching files, and asking follow-up questions.
- Connectors, projects and chat history in Claude or ChatGPT.
- Their own work tools: email, calendar, Drive, Slack, their CRM.
- That they can ask an AI how to fix an error.

## Explain

- Every terminal step, exactly: what to paste, where, and what they should see afterwards.
- Screens that look like failures but aren't, before they reach them.
- Which session or environment to use, when the app offers several.
- Rules that protect their data, stated as rules rather than reasons.

## Looks for first

What they can do with it, shown as example prompts, and how long setup takes.
